Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Draft. This is a plain-language description of what the site does today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should be before it is relied on.

What we collect

If you share an experience. The form asks for your current city and country, current status and profession, the university and subject you studied, HSC group and session, four 1–5 ratings, whether you would choose the subject again, and short written answers. It does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Please do not put personal details about yourself or other people in the written answers.

If you use the matcher. The answers you give (such as HSC group, interests and goals) are sent to our server to produce suggestions. We do not store them with your identity.

Searches and clicks. When you search, or open a result from a search, we add one to an anonymous counter for that term or result. These counters are not linked to a person or a device.

In your browser. A draft of the sharing form is saved in your browser’s local storage so you can come back to it. It is removed when you submit.

How we use it

  • Submitted experiences are held for review. Once approved they are published without your name and used to calculate subject-level statistics.
  • Matcher answers are used to generate suggestions only.
  • Search counters help order search results.

Who else handles data

  • Vercel hosts the site and stores submitted experiences and the data we maintain.
  • Anthropic receives your matcher answers together with subject data when AI-written explanations are turned on, so it can write the explanation. We ask it to use only the data we supply.
  • Google Fonts and unpkg serve fonts and code libraries, so your browser contacts them when a page loads, and they may see your IP address.

Consent, removal and contact

You give consent when you tick the box on the sharing form. To have a published response removed, email contact@edupathbd.com with the subject name and the approximate date you submitted it. We aim to remove it within 7 days. Because responses are anonymous, we may need details from you to find the right one.

Changes

If we change how data is handled we will update this page and its date.
