What EduPath BD is

EduPath BD is an independent information project that helps students in Bangladesh compare subjects, universities and careers. It is free to use. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated for any university, the University Grants Commission or any government body. University names are used only to identify programmes.

Information only, not advice

Everything on the site is general information. It is not professional, educational, financial, legal or immigration advice, and it does not guarantee admission, a job, a salary or any other outcome. Suggestions from the matcher are a starting point for your own research.

Limits of the data

  • Graduate responses are self-reported and anonymous. They are reviewed before publication but not independently verified, and for many subjects there are only a few responses.
  • Salary figures are indicative estimates from public sources and can be out of date or wrong.
  • Admission requirements, seat numbers and fees change every session. Always check the university’s official circular before you decide or apply.

If you share an experience

  • Only submit something that is true and that you wrote from your own experience.
  • Do not include names or details that identify other people, or anything defamatory, abusive or unlawful.
  • By submitting you allow us to publish your response anonymously on EduPath BD and use it in subject statistics and suggestions.
  • We may edit, decline or remove a submission. You can ask for removal by emailing contact@edupathbd.com.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the site: no automated scraping at a scale that harms it, no attempts to break or overload it, and no submitting false data to change what the site shows.

Liability

The site is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for decisions you make or losses you suffer from relying on it.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will change the date above when we do. Questions: contact@edupathbd.com.
